01 — About MEMAAR

Designing beyond appearances

Beautiful spaces.
Built to perform.

MEMAAR Design Group is an interior consulting and contracting firm established in 2016. The studio works across architecture, master planning, interior design, graphic design and turnkey fit-outs.

From private residences and bungalows to corporate offices, showrooms, retail spaces, hotels, holy places and beauty centres, every project is shaped around the client’s cultural, functional, operational and commercial needs.

“Creativity, quality and integrity — expressed through every detail.”
Our vision

To create transformative spaces that reflect individual character and elevate everyday life.

We aim to exceed expectations through thoughtful design, reliable execution, high quality and timely delivery.
